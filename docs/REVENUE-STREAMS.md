# Revenue streams for StoreForge and sibling projects

## StoreForge / BeyondMythos network

Primary revenue surfaces now supported in code:

- Affiliate links: use `AMAZON_ASSOCIATE_TAG` or `AFFILIATE_SEARCH_URL`.
- Sponsor slot: use `SPONSOR_NAME`, `SPONSOR_URL`, `SPONSOR_TAGLINE`, and `SPONSOR_LABEL`.
- Newsletter capture: send signups to `NEWSLETTER_WEBHOOK_URL` through `/api/newsletter/subscribe`.
- Digital product catalog: expand `lib/products.js` with guides, templates, prompt packs, SEO assets, and automation kits.
- External storefronts: set Etsy, Gumroad, Shopify, Payhip, Lemon Squeezy, and Ko-fi URLs.

Recommended funnel:

1. Use generated niche sites for organic discovery and newsletter capture.
2. Promote per-niche affiliate/product cards on every generated site.
3. Send newsletter subscribers to the digital product catalog and external storefronts.
4. Sell sponsor placements once the dashboard can show site count, posts, and traffic.
5. Offer custom niche-site generation as a service before buying custom domains.

## Patent / IP prep

This is not legal advice. A patent attorney should assess patentability. Before a consultation, preserve:

- Architecture diagrams of the autonomous selection, generation, deployment, and monetization loop.
- Dates of first working deployments and commits.
- Specific claims that may be novel: site inventory selection, continuous deployment lifecycle, monetization routing, per-niche offer matching, and cross-site publishing orchestration.
- Evidence that the system works across multiple independently deployed sites.

A provisional patent discussion is more plausible around the specific orchestration and monetization loop than the generic idea of AI-generated websites.
